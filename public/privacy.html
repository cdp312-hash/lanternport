What the game stores

The game has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name or email. Your Daily Trade progress, streak and stats are saved in your own browser's local storage so they're there next time you play. They never leave your device, and you can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The Lantern Cup leaderboard

If you join the Lantern Cup, our server (run on Cloudflare) stores the nickname you choose, your trading plans and the times you placed them, and a hash of a random key kept in your browser so the server can recognise you. To limit spam sign-ups it also keeps a daily count of new nicknames per network address, stored only as a one-way hash. We don't ask for your name, email or password. Your nickname and results appear on the public leaderboard. You can delete all of this at any time with "Delete my nickname and Cup data" on the Cup page.
